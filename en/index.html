RoamLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026
Last updated: August 21, 2026

RoamLens (the “App”) is a local-first travel photo memories app. This policy explains how the App handles photos and related location and time information.

1. Information We Process

After you explicitly grant photo-library permission, the App reads the following necessary metadata from the photos you allow it to access:

  • Local reference identifiers for photo-library assets;
  • The date and time a photo was taken;
  • Location coordinates embedded in a photo, when available; and
  • Country, region, and place information calculated locally on your device from those coordinates.

This information is used only to create your travel map, country and region statistics, badge progress, and local travel memories.

2. What We Do Not Process

  • We do not copy or upload your original photos.
  • We do not automatically download iCloud originals or high-resolution images.
  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not send photos, photo locations, capture times, or travel statistics to developer-operated servers.
  • We do not sell your data, serve advertising, or track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use your photo or location information to train models.

3. Local Storage and Retention

The App stores necessary photo references, capture times, coordinates, and locally calculated place information on your iPhone so that it can show your map, statistics, and badges when you return.

This local data remains until you choose “Clear Local Data” in the App’s Settings or uninstall the App. Clearing local data removes only RoamLens indexes, home-base information, and memory names. It does not delete photos or albums in Apple’s Photos app.

4. Your Choices and Controls

You can, at any time:

  • Deny, restore, or adjust RoamLens photo access in iOS Settings;
  • Choose to scan the entire photo library or selected user-created albums;
  • Scan again in the App;
  • Rename a travel memory;
  • Choose whether to create a corresponding album in Apple’s Photos app. This adds existing photos to a new album and does not copy or upload originals;
  • Clear local data in the App’s Settings; or
  • Uninstall the App to remove data in its sandbox.

If you revoke photo access, the App cannot read new photo metadata. You can remove existing local indexes using “Clear Local Data.”

5. Map Services

The App uses Apple MapKit to display maps. Map services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policy. RoamLens does not upload your photos, original images, or travel statistics to developer-operated servers.

6. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

RoamLens v0.1 does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, accounts, cloud sync, or developer-operated servers. Other than using Apple MapKit to display maps, the App does not share the photo metadata described above with third parties.

If we add a third-party SDK, cloud sync, analytics, or accounts in the future, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before enabling them.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not create accounts or collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently processed information that should not have been processed, please contact the developer below.

8. Policy Updates

If our data practices materially change, we will update this policy before releasing a new version and update the App Store privacy information.
